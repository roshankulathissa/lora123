CV writing tips specifically for applying to jobs in Romania
 -|  Keep It Concise and Clear
 -|  Limit your CV to 1 to 2 pages
 -|  Focus on relevant experience that matches the Romanian job market (for example, construction, caregiving, hospitality, or IT).
 -|  Highlight Work Experience
 -|  List your experience in reverse chronological order (most recent first).
     Include:
     * Company name and location (mention if it was abroad)
     * Job title
     * Duration (month/year – month/year)
     * Main duties and achievements

 -|  Include Education and Certifications
     * Mention diplomas, technical courses, or professional training.
     * If your certificates are translated into Romanian or English, mention it.
     * Include recognized qualifications like  NVQ, City & Guilds, or trade-specific certificates if you’re applying for skilled labor jobs.

 -|  Language Skills Matter
     * Clearly state your language proficiency
 -|  Emphasize Technical and Soft Skills
     * For skilled jobs: include your technical abilities (e.g., machinery operation, electrical wiring, carpentry).
     * For service roles: highlight communication, teamwork, and responsibility.

 -|  Add a Professional Photo
     * In Romania, CVs often include a passport-size photo (neutral background, professional appearance).
     * Avoid casual or selfie-style photos.

 -|  Personal Information
   Include:
    * Full name
    * Date of birth or age
    * Nationality
    * Contact details (phone, email)
    * Current location and willingness to relocate

 -|  Optional: marital status (still common in Romania, but not required).
